# Changelog

All notable changes to this integration are documented here.

## v1.3.0

### Added
- **A route's detail lookups have a switch and a pace of their own.** **Look up the reports' details**, on a route's form and under **TomTom Settings → Configure → Defaults for new routes**, decides whether Incident Details is asked for the rest of a new report at all - the whole stretch, where it starts and ends, the road numbers, TomTom's wording and its times. Off, a route costs no Incident Details request, and its reports show what the route itself names: kind, place and delay. **Minutes between detail lookups** under it spaces the lookups apart from the route's own polls: with 30, a new report shows at once and its details follow within half an hour - several reports to a request, and none for a jam that has gone again meanwhile. On, and with every poll, by default - what a route did before.
- **A limit on the day's Incident Details requests.** **Incident Details requests a day, at most**, under **TomTom Settings → Configure → Quota warning → TomTom**, counts every entry together from midnight to midnight UTC. Once it is reached nothing more is sent until the next day, and a repair **The day's Incident Details requests are used** says so, with the time it ends in your time zone; it goes away by itself. **80** by default, which makes the 2,500 free requests last the month, and **0** for no limit. The default applies to an installation from before as well: one that sends more a day - a single area polled every 15 minutes is 96 - reaches the limit until the number is raised.
- **The card says when something is missing.** A line above the list, and above the pop-up's: while the entry's last update failed and what is shown is from before, while an area or a tracker goes without TomTom reports because no Incident Details requests are left, and while a route's new reports come without their details for the same reason.
- **`incident_details_ok` on Reports, `used_up` on Quota at risk.** The first is false while an entry goes without Incident Details - none left at TomTom, or the day's limit reached. **Quota at risk** is on through both, lists the APIs TomTom calls used up as `used_up`, and carries `incident_details_day_limit` and `incident_details_day_limit_reached`.

### Changed
- **A used-up quota is named for what it is, and the entries keep running.** TomTom counts its free tier per API, and answers the one that has no requests left with *insufficient funds*. That used to read "TomTom refused the API key" and took every entry down with it, routes and travel times included. Now a repair **TomTom has no Incident Details requests left** names the API and the month's count, and only what needs that API is missing: an area or a tracker shows no TomTom reports - the further sources' speed cameras and the traffic flow stay - and a route's new reports come as the route itself names them. The API is asked again with the next request there is for it, no sooner than an hour after the refusal; the first answer, the turn of the month or a new key clears it. Reports that return with it are not announced as new. Without Routing a route cannot be worked out: its poll still fails, with that reason.
- **A route looks up only the reports it shows.** A kind switched off, a jam below **Jam from this delay** and a blacklisted report cost a detail lookup each, to be left out afterwards. They are now left out before it.
- **The minimal format's editor offers what its pop-up draws.** **Layout → Traffic button** and **Layout → Info line** are there in the minimal format too, since the pop-up draws both with those settings - the info line without its symbol, which stands under no travel time. Under the pop-up's **Show the travel time** there are switches for **Show the refresh button** and **Traffic's share (live route)**, and **Layout → Refresh button** goes with the travel time, the one place that format draws the button. What the format has no use for is hidden while it is chosen and kept as saved: **Offer the area dropdown**, **Offer "New route" in the dropdown**, **List follows the map**, and **Centre of the map view** under **Measure distance from**.
- **Saving the quota warning or the defaults for new routes no longer polls every entry.** Both live on the TomTom Settings entry, and any save there fetched every area and route again - a request each, for a changed percentage. Only a change to the data sources still does.
- **Home Assistant 2024.12 or newer.** The options dialogs have needed it all along; the README and HACS said 2024.7.
- **The way back is named in your language.** Its suggested name ended in "return" in every language but one; it now ends in the word the form's own help text uses.
- **The blueprint names both sources.** Fixed, mobile and red-light cameras and section controls read "(blitzer.de, Lufop)" in the Report Alerts blueprint's list of kinds.

### Fixed
- **Speed cameras are no longer reported as new after a restart.** blitzer.de and Lufop are asked a moment after TomTom, and an entry's first list - TomTom's alone - was taken for the whole: every camera's entity was removed and made again at each restart and each reload, announced with a `tomtom_new_report` event of its own, and under a new entity id - `_2`, `_3` - wherever the old one was still held by a leftover state. The entities now wait for the sources to answer, keep their ids, and nothing is announced that was there before.
- **A report without details no longer shows its delay as its place.** A route's report whose lookup had not come round printed "+2 min" on the place line, behind the pin.
- **A route's hours past midnight belong to the day they began on.** Monday to Friday from 22:00 to 06:00 started on Monday at midnight and stopped on Friday at midnight; it now starts on Monday at 22:00 and runs into Saturday morning.
- **A failed detail lookup no longer fails the route.** A lookup TomTom did not answer took the whole poll down, travel time included. The route now goes on and asks again later; only a refused key still fails it.
- **Jam from this delay is kept among the defaults for new routes.** The number was saved and never read back: the form showed 0 again, and no new route got it.
- **A report seen without details keeps the moment it was first seen.** A restart set it to now, and the report counted as new again.
- **A form shown again after an error keeps what was typed** - the area, the route's last step, the data sources, an address and a zone.
- **A source's repair clears when the source is switched off**, and with its first answer after a reload.
- **An area searched in several boxes keeps the boxes already answered** when TomTom refuses a later one.
- **A reload in the middle of a poll no longer costs the request gate a place.** Each cancelled request left one fewer of the four in flight.
- **`tomtom.refresh` on the TomTom Settings entry says what is wrong**: the entry holds the settings and has nothing to refresh.
- **The card reads a report's time in Home Assistant's time zone.** A browser in another zone took the stamp for its own and got the age wrong - for NEW, for the chips and for sorting by newest or oldest. With **Time zone** in the profile on the server's, the times are also written in it.
- **"Just now" and NEW age between polls.** The card redraws them on a clock; they used to stand still until the next poll or click.
- **A live route's colours follow every poll.** The stretches traffic is slowing were fetched again only when the number of reports or the route itself changed.
- **The pop-up's map keeps its scale bar and its traffic flow** when it is rebuilt while open.
- **The route is fitted into view again** when the card comes back to the page - after a change of view, say.
- **Numbers in the `1'234.56` format.** The profile's number format with the apostrophe was written in the language's own.
- **An emptied number field in the editor is the default again**, not the field's minimum.
- **A language that failed to load is fetched again** after half a minute; the card stayed in English until the page was reloaded.
- **The planner**: a preview is dropped once a place is changed, so **Save as route** no longer saves the route from before; a failed address search says so under its field; the suggested name follows the places; a saved route is confirmed; a name the way back cannot have is reported; the crosshair for the map's centre comes back; a card without a map does not offer the map's centre; and the arrow between the places points the right way in Arabic and Hebrew.
- **A card updated in an open page keeps what other extensions added to it** - card-mod's styles, for one.

## v1.2.1

### Fixed
- **The colour menus in the card editor stay in view.** The menu beside every colour field opens towards the side with more room and is capped to it. Which room that was, the card measured against the first scrolling box above the editor - and in some dialogs that is not the box that actually clips the menu. Opened upwards near the top of a block, the menu then ran off the top of the dialog, with its first entries out of reach. It now measures against every box that really clips it, and keeps at least room for about three entries.

## v1.2.0

### Added
- **An updated card reaches every open page by itself.** A dashboard that is open while Home Assistant restarts is not served again, and the dashboards' resources are read once per page - so a new version of the card used to show up only after a reload, and only after a hard one where a service worker kept the old file. The card's loader, which stays in the page, now asks the integration after every reconnect which version it serves and fetches a newer one into the page; the new file takes over the registered elements and redraws every card and editor on the page, with what they show and the editor's open form. No reload, no message. The first installation is the one page that cannot do this - it holds nothing of the card yet - and still needs the page loaded once.
- **The planner's preview carries the speed cameras too.** A route calculated on the card used to preview TomTom's reports alone; with blitzer.de or Lufop switched on, their cameras and checks along the route now join the list, the map, the badges and the bar - asked with the kinds the defaults for new routes switch on for each source, so the preview shows what the route gets once saved.
- **The planned route shows its traffic flow.** The line is coloured the way the flow tiles colour the roads - green where traffic moves freely, and over that every stretch TomTom rates in yellow, orange or red by how badly it moves there, grey where it is closed - inside the plan's orange edge, so it still reads as the planned route. A saved live route is drawn the same way, with its own colour from **Layout → Map** as the edge - on the card's map and on the minimal format's pop-up.
- **A Google Maps button on the map.** **Show the Google Maps button** under **Settings → Map** puts Google's pin top right on the map - and on the minimal format's pop-up map. A click opens the entry in Google Maps in a new tab: a route with its start, stops and destination in the route planner, an area or a tracker at its centre, zoomed to the circle. Off by default.
- **A switch per source under the kinds both sources answer.** Fixed, mobile and red-light cameras and section controls come from blitzer.de and from Lufop; each of those four is a section of its own below the kinds list of an entry - and of the route defaults under TomTom Settings - with a switch for **blitzer.de** and one for **Lufop** and none for the kind itself, so a route outside Lufop's countries can leave Lufop out and spend none of its day's requests, while blitzer.de still reports the kind. Both on by default; an entry from before keeps both.

### Fixed
- **The planner counts one report in the singular.** A calculated route with a single report said "1 reports"; the count now follows each language's plural rule, with a singular of its own where the language has one.
- **The editor's tooltips show inside a Bubble Card pop-up editor.** Opened from the pencil in Bubble Card's pop-up editor, the page under the dialogs ends up scrolled away; the card then took the page's body, an overflow-hidden box far above the window, for something that clips the dialog, and hid every tooltip for lying outside it. A fixed dialog is not clipped by the page it floats over, and the tooltips now know that.
- **The bar of kinds hides a planned route's symbols too.** While a route was being previewed, a click on a kind in the bar took the kind out of the list but left its symbols on the map; the planner's symbols are the card's own, and they now follow the bar like the entry's.
- **A forced map theme colours the map's overlays too.** With **Layout → Map → Map theme** on light over a dark dashboard, the symbols' group bubbles, the scale bar and the Google Maps tile kept the dashboard's dark colours on the light map; they now take the map's - and the other way round.
- **A restart no longer leaves one entry "initialising" for minutes.** A free TomTom key may make five requests a second, over every API together, and a restart started every entry at once: whichever request crossed the line got a 429, which this integration read as the month's quota being used up and which sent that entry into Home Assistant's retry loop - three minutes for the unlucky one, a different one each time. Every TomTom request of the installation now passes one gate, four in flight and four a second at most; a 429 that still gets through is tried once more after the pause TomTom asks for, and failing that is named for what it is: **TomTom refuses more requests right now**, not a used-up quota.

### Changed
- **The Traffic button moved beside the refresh button.** It used to stand alone under the entry dropdown as "Traffic flow" in capitals; it now follows the refresh pill in the info line - or the big travel time of a live route - in the pill's shape, reads "Traffic" with the jam's symbol before it.
- **Every pill centres its symbol and its word.** The refresh pill, the Traffic button, the NEW and delay badges, the planner's buttons, the NEW tag, the chips and the bar of kinds: each used to stand on a text baseline, so a bigger symbol grew a pill upwards only. Now symbol and word sit on the pill's middle whatever their sizes - the word trimmed to its capitals where the browser can.
- **The Traffic button has the refresh button's Layout options.** Under **Layout → Traffic button**: its symbol with colour and size, its text colour while shown, **Show background** for the pill - off leaves symbol and word on the card, coloured while shown and grey while hidden - its background colour and its font.
- **One look for every badge and button.** The NEW and delay badges, the refresh pill, the Traffic button and the planner's buttons all start in capitals and not bold; the pop-up's NEW tag too. A card that set either itself keeps its setting. The Reported and Confirmed chips stay as they were.
- **A source that stops answering no longer leaves stale reports behind.** blitzer.de and Lufop are optional sources, so a failure never took an entry down - but what the source last brought stayed on the cards, in the entities and in the speed camera counts for as long as it was gone, looking current. Now a source that fails three polls in a row has its reports dropped until it answers again, a repair **{source} is not answering** says so, and `sources_ok` on **All speed cameras** and **New speed cameras** reads false meanwhile.

## v1.1.0

### Added
- **A link to every report.** `url` opens a report from blitzer.de on blitzer.de's map, and `blitzer_key` carries blitzer.de's own id for it. A report from TomTom links to TomTom's route planner, centred on its stretch and zoomed to fit it: the planner has no page for a single report, but shows it there with its traffic info on. The link names no language, so the planner opens in the reader's. Lufop has no page for a radar, so its reports carry none. For a notification, or a Markdown card.
- **The card's map can open that link.** **Map click opens the report**, under the map's settings beside the highlight, makes a click on a report's symbol open its link in a new tab. Off by default; a click on a Lufop report does what it did. Without Lufop every report has a page, so the editor then shows **Map click highlights the entry** off and greyed out - what is saved for it is kept.
- **A symbol and a colour per kind of report.** Under **Layout → General → Kinds of report** the card's editor lists every kind TomTom and the sources switched on can report, each with a symbol and a colour of the card's own - on the map's marker, the band along the road, the list and the bar. Empty keeps the integration's symbol and today's colours.
- **A bar of the kinds on the map.** **Show the bar of kinds** puts one symbol per kind the map holds between map and list - under the map, or beside it side by side, and in the minimal format's pop-up too - with how many there are. A click hides that kind on the map and in the list, and from the badges; a second brings it back. Remembered in this browser for this card.
- **Two counts of the speed cameras.** **All speed cameras** and **New speed cameras** on every entry count the reports from blitzer.de and Lufop among the ones the entry delivers, and those of them reported inside the entry's "new" window - the same reports **Reports** counts and the card shows, so the new count is the card's badge; it ages out on its own, once a minute. Per source and per kind as attributes, `in_range` for the number beyond the cap; a camera both sources report counts once.
- **The map's own theme.** **Map theme** under **Layout → Map** sets the map light or dark whatever the dashboard's theme - a light map on a dark dashboard, say. **Automatic**, the default, follows Home Assistant's theme as before. The pop-up's map follows the same setting.
- **A jam from this much delay.** **Jam from this delay**, under an entry's **Optional settings** below the kinds, leaves out the jams whose delay TomTom puts below that many minutes - the thirty-second ones that make a commute look worse than it is. 0, the default, reports every jam as before.
- **The stars can be switched off.** blitzer.de's confirmation stars in a list entry have a switch of their own under **Settings → List**, beside **Reported at** and **Until when**; they used to go with **Reported at**. The three switch their own part only, and the stars' switch is there only while blitzer.de is one of the sources.
- **The refresh button stays with the info line off.** It answers to its own switch alone and stands under the title on its own when the line is off; it used to go with the line.

### Changed
- **TomTom's quota is warned of in two stages, like Lufop's day.** The first repair comes when the month's projection reaches the share set under **Quota warning → TomTom**, the second when it reaches the whole free tier. The share starts at 80 % on a new installation; one from before keeps the 100 % it had. The dialog's two sections open folded.
- **Reports counts what it delivers.** The `new` attribute and the counts per kind on **Reports** are counted over the delivered reports - the nearest, up to the entry's count - as the state is, so they agree with the card's badge and the camera sensors; the number in range beyond the cap is the new `in_range` attribute.

### Fixed
- **Every blitzer.de camera gets its entity.** blitzer.de files a second camera at one spot under an id ending in `-T1`; the integration took that ending for the id, and every such camera became one entity - an 18 km circle around Munich delivered 49 reports where its count said 60. The card showed those 49, and its badge counted the new among them. The id is now what follows blitzer.de's leading figure: `94657` as before, `98878-T1` for those.

## v1.0.2

### Added
- **Police reports from blitzer.de.** A kind of its own, `police_report`: the traffic bulletins of the police that blitzer.de passes on - "A8, Salzburg - München, zwischen Tunnel Neubiberg und München-Perlach in beiden Richtungen Gefahr durch Gegenstände auf der Fahrbahn". The road and the town are the place, the bulletin is in `description`, and the symbol is a megaphone in the red of every report from a further source. On like every kind, so an entry with blitzer.de switched on brings them at its next poll, and the Report Alerts blueprint can filter on them. They are asked in a request of their own: together with the cameras, blitzer.de folds them into groups the search does not resolve.

### Changed
- **The symbol at the start of a row has the colour of its marker on the map.** It was the text colour for TomTom's kinds and red for the sources' only; now every kind's symbol is coloured alike in the list and on the map - dark red for a closed road, orange for roadworks and a closed lane, red for a jam, amber for the rest, and the sources' red - so the list reads like the map.

### Fixed
- **blitzer.de's cameras were missing from a wide search.** A box wider than about 10 km comes back from blitzer.de with its cameras gathered into groups, and a group was asked again in a box of a tenth of the search's radius around its centre - which held none of its members when they lay further out than that. Measured against a 2 km grid of the same area: 2 of 3 cameras lost in a 12 km circle, 14 of 52 in an 18 km one. A group is now asked again in a quarter of the radius, which found every camera of the grid in both, in the same number of requests.
- **The NEW badge sits in the middle of its line.** It was centred on the line's lower-case letters, a pixel under the capitals and digits a report's line starts with. It now sits on the middle of the capitals.
- **A stretch's length is written with the right decimal separator.** The `summary` - and with it every notification from the blueprint - wrote it with a point in every language, "Stau · 1.2 km"; it now follows the installation's language, "1,2 km" in German. On the card it follows each reader's own number format, the same as the distance in front of it, so one line no longer reads "1,5 km · Stau · 0.2 km".

## v1.0.1

### Fixed
- **A blueprint you changed is no longer overwritten.** The Report Alerts blueprint was written over on every start whenever the copy under `config/blueprints/automation/tomtom/` differed from the shipped one. The integration now remembers what it last installed: a missing copy is installed, an untouched one is updated when a release changes the blueprint, and one you edited is left alone, with a line in the log when a newer version ships.
- **The traffic flow tiles compare their token in constant time.** The tile path checks its per-start token with `hmac.compare_digest` and refuses every request while no token is set.

## v1.0.0

Initial release: TomTom traffic reports for an area, a device tracker or a live route as `geo_location` entities, the travel time of a route, and a dashboard card of its own. Everything it does is described in the [README](README.md).
